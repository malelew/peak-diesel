# Peak Diesel

The price of diesel in the U.S. reached a new nominal high recently, but I've been curious if and when we will hit the highest recorded inflation-adjusted price for diesel. According to data from the [U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA)]((https://www.eia.gov/)), diesel prices hit their all-time high the week of May 26, 2008 at $4.723 a gallon. Adjusting for inflation, that would cost $7.30 today.
