# Peak Diesel

The price of diesel in the U.S. has its all-time nominal high, but I've been curious if and when we will hit the highest recorded inflation-adjusted price for diesel. According to data from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, diesel prices hit their all-time high the week of May 26, 2008 at $4.723 a gallon. Adjusting for inflation, that would be $7.30 today.
